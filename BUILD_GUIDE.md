# Build Guide

This is an Autodesk Inventor workflow for a comparable pumpjack demonstrator. The portfolio does not specify dimensions, so set and record a scale.

1. Define the base, A-frame, beam pivot, drive axis, and crank center.
2. Build the base and support first. Model the walking beam and curved horsehead, then the drive wheel, shaft, crank, and connecting link. Keep center distances and crank offset as named parameters.
3. Ground the base and attach the frame. Allow rotation at the beam pivot and drive shaft. Connect the crank, pitman, and beam without locking the mechanism.
4. Drive the crank through one full cycle. Check for collision around the frame, beam, and linkage.
5. Produce front and isometric views, an assembly drawing, and a parts list.

Mark geometry chosen for illustration. A CAD demonstrator cannot be described as a complete oil rig or deployable pump without relevant engineering analysis.
